Session 1 — Parts of a Plant

Plants Around Us · Grade 3 · Environmental Studies · 40-minute session

Chapter Progress So Far: This is the first session of the chapter. Today the children meet Meena and learn the five parts of a plant.
📋 Part One — Before Class

Learning Outcomes

  1. Name the five parts of a plant — root, stem, leaf, flower, fruit.
  2. Say what each part does, in one simple sentence.
  3. Point out these parts on a real plant or a clear picture.
  4. Explain why a plant needs its roots and leaves to stay alive.
  5. Handle a real plant gently, as a living thing.

Anticipated Misconceptions (three)

Child saysWhyTeacher does
"The roots are dirty, they do nothing." Roots are hidden. Show roots drinking in a clear glass of water.
"Only the flower matters." Flowers are bright. Cover the flower, then the leaves — ask if the plant can still live.
"The stem is just a stick." It looks plain. Trace the water's path up the stem with a finger.

📖 The Story · Meena and Paati's Garden

In a village near Madurai, where the morning smells of jasmine and filter coffee, lived a girl named Meena. Every evening she helped her grandmother — her Paati — in the little garden behind their house…

So a plant has many parts, and each one has its own job, Meena whispered. That night she wondered: how many kinds of plants were waiting in the village for her to meet tomorrow?
