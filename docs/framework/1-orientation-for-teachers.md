# Your New Lesson Planning Assistant

> **Document 1 of 4 · Read this first · For teachers**
>
> A short orientation — please read this once before you begin.

## What is this?

Claude — an AI assistant — will now create your lesson plans for you. You upload the chapter from your textbook, answer five small questions, and you receive complete, ready-to-teach session plans built for your grade, your subject, and your chapter. It takes the writing and structuring off your shoulders, so your energy goes into what matters — understanding the lesson and delivering it well.

## What will you receive?

For every chapter: a session-by-session breakdown for your approval, a colourful mind map of the full lesson for your children, a complete plan for each session, a visual recap chart to show at the end of every class, a ready-made Google Classroom post for each evening, and a one-page chapter summary at the end — handy for revision before assessments.

## What does a session plan look like?

**Before class** — what to prepare: the outcomes, the content, the questions children might ask, the materials to gather, and a short self-check. Prepare well, and the lesson lives in you — not on paper.

**During class** — one clean sequence with timings. Connect to what children know, teach the key ideas, do an activity, check their learning — then the heart of every session: the story, where the concepts come alive through a character your children follow across the whole chapter. Every class closes with a short Takeaway.

**After class** — a few minutes of reflection, and the Evening Post.

## How does the plan flex with your day?

- **Core** — the six essentials, about twenty-five minutes.
- **Enhanced** — the essentials plus enriching extras, about thirty-five minutes. This is your normal.
- **Full** — everything, the complete forty minutes.

You choose your level while preparing. During class, if you are running behind, only the Story and the Takeaway always happen — nothing skipped is lost, as it reaches your children that evening through the Google Classroom post. You just copy, paste, and post.

## What stays entirely yours?

The teaching. How you tell the story, how you answer the unexpected question, what you skip and what you savour — all yours. The plan is the map; you are the journey.

## Your routine for every chapter

1. Generate all session plans at least a week before the chapter begins
2. Before each class — prepare from Part One and do the short self-check
3. Teach
4. After class — reflect briefly and paste the Evening Post

We built this so every child gets a thoughtfully designed lesson — and every teacher the time to deliver it wonderfully.
