# How to Use the Lesson Plan System

> **Document 2 of 4 · Read this second · For teachers**
>
> Your step-by-step guide. Keep this handy until the steps feel natural.

## Before you start — what you need

- The **Lesson Plan Generation Framework** file your school gave you. Save it somewhere easy to reach — you use it for every chapter.
- The **lesson PDF** — the scanned pages of the chapter you are planning.
- Your **Claude account**, already set up for you by the school.

## The five small questions you will answer

- **Chapter Number** — for example, Chapter 14
- **Chapter Name** — for example, Plants Around Us
- **Grade** — for example, Grade 3
- **Subject** — English, Maths, Environmental Studies, or Social Studies
- **Total Sessions** — how many sessions you are planning for this chapter

## Step by step — generating your plans

1. Open a new chat in Claude. One chapter, one conversation.
2. Upload both files. The Framework first, then the lesson PDF, in the same message.
3. Type your five small questions and send.
4. Confirm the chapter. Claude tells you what it found; if it is right, reply yes.
5. Look at the Session Map. If it suits your class, reply confirmed; if not, say what to change. If Claude suggests a different number of sessions, it is only a suggestion — you decide.
6. Ask for each session, one at a time. Reply yes; download each session's PDF as it comes.
7. Collect your files. Keep each session PDF, and the complete chapter file at the end.

## Continuing later

You need not make all the sessions in one sitting — but always come back to the same conversation to continue.

To find it again: open Claude and look at your list of past conversations — down the left on a computer, under the menu on a phone. Your chapter will be there by name. Open it and type yes, generate the next session.

## What you will receive for every chapter

- A **Chapter Learning Map** — a colourful picture of the whole lesson, posted to Google Classroom at the start
- A **plan for every session** — each as its own PDF to download and keep
- **Today's Learning Chart** — inside each session, shown in class and sent home
- An **Evening Post** — inside each session, ready to copy and paste into Google Classroom
- A **Chapter Summary** — at the end, for revision before any test
- **One complete chapter file** — everything together in a single PDF

## Your simple rhythm for every chapter

1. Generate all your sessions about a week before you start teaching
2. Before each class, prepare from Part One and run the short self-check at the end of the plan — a quick readiness check so you walk in confident
3. Teach
4. After class, reflect briefly and post the Evening Post to Google Classroom

## One gentle reminder

Keep your conversations about lessons and chapters. There is no need to type any child's name, marks, or personal details into Claude — the system works perfectly well without them.

The first chapter may take a little patience as you find your way; by the second, it will feel easy.
